# Service Content Review

Status: Internal clinician/client approval checklist

The public service-detail pages use the supplied November 5, 2026 draft only for general educational information. They deliberately avoid confirming that Falola Psychiatry Practice provides any specific assessment, medication, psychotherapy, coaching format, referral, or level of care.

## Confirmation required before production approval

1. Confirm that all ten services are active and correctly named.
2. Confirm whether every service is limited to adults and whether any other eligibility limits apply.
3. Confirm which services are available in person, by telepsychiatry, or both.
4. Confirm the assessments, medication management, psychotherapy modalities, monitoring, and referrals actually available for each service.
5. Confirm whether exposure and response prevention is offered for OCD; the page currently describes it only as a general evidence-based option.
6. Confirm addiction-care boundaries, including detoxification, withdrawal management, medications for substance use disorders, opioid treatment, and referral arrangements. None are represented as offered.
7. Confirm the formats, modalities, number of sessions, and eligibility for Brief Psychotherapies.
8. Confirm who provides Academic Coaching, eligible age groups, delivery format, and whether it is separate from psychiatric treatment.
9. Approve the service-specific appointment language, educational disclaimers, emergency guidance, and public source links.
10. Clinically review and approve every page before deployment to a public production environment.

## Statements intentionally omitted

- Treatment guarantees, outcomes, or success rates.
- Claims that Dr. Falola personally provides a named therapy, assessment, medication service, detoxification service, or coaching program.
- Insurance coverage or service-specific reimbursement claims.
- Diagnostic conclusions based on symptoms listed on a page.
- Medication prescribing policies and service-specific telepsychiatry eligibility.
- Patient intake questions or forms collecting medical information.
